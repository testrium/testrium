# Testrium Trademark and Attribution Policy

Testrium's source code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE.txt). The license covers the **code**. It does **not** cover the **Testrium name, logo or brand** (Apache License 2.0, Section 6). This policy explains what you may do with the brand and how to credit Testrium when you build on it.

## Attribution is required

If you distribute Testrium or any work based on it (a fork, a modified build, a rebranded product, a Docker image, or a bundled or hosted offering), you must:

1. **Keep the [`LICENSE.txt`](./LICENSE.txt) and [`NOTICE`](./NOTICE) files** in your distribution (Apache 2.0, Sections 4(a) and 4(d)).
2. **Keep the existing copyright and attribution notices** in the source files (Section 4(c)).
3. **Mark the files you changed** with a prominent notice that you changed them (Section 4(b)).
4. **Credit Testrium in a place users can see it**, such as your README, documentation, or the app's About/Credits screen. For example:

   > Based on [Testrium](https://github.com/testrium/testrium), licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

Removing the NOTICE attribution or passing off Testrium's work as entirely your own breaks the license, and your right to use the code ends under its terms.

## What you may do

- Use, modify and self-host Testrium for any purpose, including commercial use.
- Say truthfully that your product is "based on Testrium", "a fork of Testrium" or "compatible with Testrium".
- Use the name "Testrium" in articles, tutorials, reviews and talks about the project.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Call a modified or derivative product "Testrium", or use a confusingly similar name (for example "Testrium Pro" or "Testrium Cloud").
- Use the Testrium logo or visual branding for your own product, service, company or domain name.
- Suggest that your product, fork or service is official, endorsed, certified or supported by the Testrium project.
- Sell or offer a hosted service under the Testrium name.

If you fork Testrium to build your own tool, **give it a new name and logo** and credit Testrium as described above.

## Asking for permission or reporting misuse

To ask for permission or report misuse, open an issue at <https://github.com/testrium/testrium/issues>.
